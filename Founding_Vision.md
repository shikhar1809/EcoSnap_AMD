# EcoSnap (Manifesto)

> "EcoSnap is the obvious next step for anyone who wants to go green."

## The Core Loop: "Snap -> Recommend -> Service -> Points"

1.  📸 **Snap (The Hook)**
    The entry point is effortless. Point the camera at anything—a broken toaster, a plastic bottle, a room.
    *Key Tech: Smart Vision & Forensic AI.*

2.  💡 **Recommend (The Value)**
    Immediate, verified insight. "This bottle can be recycled for ₹10" or "Repair this toaster, don't replace it."
    *Key Tech: Verified Data Layer.*

3.  🛠️ **Service (The Solution)**
    Don't just diagnose; fix. Direct links to **Top Picks** (Eco-marketplace), **Subsidies**, or **Repair Guides**.
    *Key Tech: TopPicks & Subsidy Modules.*

4.  🔥 **Points (The Reward)**
    Gamify the good deed. Streaks, Tiers (Circular Hero), and Social Proof.
    *Key Tech: Gamification Engine.*

## Target Audience: Gen-Z
The "Snap & Solve" mechanic is designed for instant gratification and high scalability. It abstracts the complexity of sustainability into a single tap.
