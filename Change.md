## **WHAT IS SCANCARBON?**

### **The Core Idea**
```
User: Takes photo of ANY product
     ↓
App: Uses ML to identify product + brand + model
     ↓
Database: Retrieves comprehensive sustainability data
     ↓
Returns:
├─ Carbon footprint (cradle-to-grave)
├─ Recycling time (by location/material)
├─ Material breakdown + safety scores
├─ Reusability potential
├─ Circular economy rating (A-F)
├─ Alternative products (lower carbon)
├─ Carbon credits (if recycled)
└─ ESG impact visualization
```

**Like Shazam for products. But for sustainability.**
