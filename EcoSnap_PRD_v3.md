## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EcoSnap is a **barrier-breaking green tech adoption platform** that solves 15 critical problems preventing people from going green. It's not just about appliances—it's about removing EVERY friction point in the sustainability journey.

**Core Thesis:**
- People WANT to go green (83% awareness)
- But they DON'T because barriers exist (cost, trust, complexity, inconvenience)
- EcoSnap removes ALL barriers → Conversion happens naturally

**Target Users:**
- Gen Z (18-25): Price-sensitive, FOMO-driven, digital-first
- Young professionals (25-35): Environmentally aware, pragmatic
- Indian urban population: Cost-conscious, peer-influenced, practical

## PART 1: GENERAL GREEN TECH PROBLEMS (Beyond Appliances)

### PROBLEM 1: The Intention-Action Gap (The Core Problem)
**Research Finding:** 83% of Gen Z aware of sustainability. But only 16% actually act.

**Why:** People know they SHOULD go green, but don't know WHERE to start or HOW.

**EcoSnap Solution:**
- Snap ANY green product/appliance → Get personalized action plan
- No decision paralysis, no overwhelm
- Clear: What to do, why to do it, how to do it

**Metrics:**
- Snap products: AC, solar, EV chargers, LED bulbs, water heaters, insulation, smart thermostats
- Get instant: Cost, savings, payback, impact
- Book installation OR purchase: In-app
